Vocal Practice

Find out where your voice is right now

9 short steps, about 10 minutes. You'll get a report card with your range, likely voice type, what you're already good at, what to work on, and a practice plan.

  • Speaking voice: helps estimate your voice type
  • Lowest and highest notes: your range
  • Match 3 notes: pitch accuracy and how steady you hold a note
  • Echo 2 short tunes: musical memory
  • Listening test: how small a pitch difference you can hear

Find a quiet spot. There are no wrong answers: this just sets your starting point so you can see yourself improve. Never push through pain or strain.

For each step, tap Record live. If your device blocks the mic, use Upload recording: record a short voice memo (or a quick video, since only the sound is used) and pick it.

Guided warm-ups

A chord cues each key, then sing the pattern. It climbs a half step each time through your range. Start easy, on "ah", "mee" or a lip trill.
